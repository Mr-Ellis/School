Lesson 5 · Printers
Year 10 · Hardware · How computers produce hard copies

Learning Objectives

  • Understand what a printer is and why it is used
  • Understand the differences between inkjet, laser and 3D printers
  • Understand the advantages and disadvantages of each printer type
  • Understand the purpose of actuators in printers
💡 Key Fact: Printers are output devices that produce a physical (hard copy) version of digital documents, images or 3D objects.

🖨️ Inkjet Printers

  • Spray tiny droplets of liquid ink onto paper through microscopic nozzles.
  • Use actuators to control the ink droplets – piezoelectric crystals change shape when voltage is applied, forcing ink out.
  • Can produce high-quality colour images and photos.
  • Advantages: Low initial cost, good for photos, can print on various media.
  • Disadvantages: Ink can be expensive, slower than laser printers, ink can smudge.
  • Common uses: Home printing, photos, school projects, small offices.

⚡ Laser Printers

  • Use a laser beam to create an electrostatic image on a rotating drum.
  • Toner (fine powder) is attracted to the charged areas, then fused onto paper using heat and pressure.
  • Use actuators in the paper feed mechanism and toner cartridge.
  • Advantages: Fast, high-volume printing, sharp text, toner doesn't smudge.
  • Disadvantages: Higher initial cost, colour laser printers are expensive, toner can be costly.
  • Common uses: Offices, schools, high-volume document printing.

🔧 3D Printers

  • Create three-dimensional objects by building layers of material (plastic, resin, metal).
  • Use actuators to precisely position the print head and control material flow.
  • Common types: FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling) – melts plastic filament; SLA (Stereolithography) – uses UV light to cure resin.
  • Advantages: Can create complex shapes, rapid prototyping, custom parts.
  • Disadvantages: Slow, expensive, limited materials, post-processing required.
  • Common uses: Prototyping, engineering, medical models, jewellery, education.

🔁 Plenary – Which Printer?

Discuss the answers to the following questions with a partner:

  1. What is the main difference between how an inkjet and a laser printer work?
  2. Why might a business choose a laser printer over an inkjet printer?
  3. What is the role of an actuator in a printer?
Click to reveal suggested answers
  • Inkjet vs Laser: Inkjet sprays liquid ink through nozzles; laser uses a laser beam to create an electrostatic image on a drum and transfers toner (powder) fused with heat.
  • Business choice: Laser printers are faster, have lower running costs for high volumes, and produce sharper text – ideal for offices.
  • Actuator role: Actuators control the ink droplets in inkjet printers (piezoelectric crystals) and manage paper feed mechanisms and toner transfer in laser printers.
