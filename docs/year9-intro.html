Year 9 Course Breakdown
Term 1, 2 & 3 · Full Syllabus Breakdown

Course Aims

Students will develop confidence in computational thinking and programming. They will understand the main principles of solving problems using computers, the component parts of computer systems, the internet, and automated technologies.

Duration: 38 Weeks (3 Terms)

Topics Not Covered in Year 9

Based on the 2026-2028 syllabus, the following topics are not included in the Year 9 Course Plan and will need to be taught in later years:

  • Topic 6: Automated and Emerging Technologies (Robotics, AI, Expert Systems)
  • Topic 7: Algorithm design and problem-solving
  • Topic 8: Programming
  • Topic 9: Databases
  • Topic 10: Boolean logic
Exam Information (0478)

2026-2028 Assessment Overview

  • Paper 1 - Computer Systems 1 hour 45 mins | 75 marks (50%)
    Covers Topics 1-6 (Short-answer and structured questions).
  • Paper 2 - Algorithms, Programming & Logic 1 hour 45 mins | 75 marks (50%)
    Covers Topics 7-10 (Includes a 15-mark scenario question).
  • NO Calculators permitted Candidates are expected to be able to perform binary and hex conversions manually.
