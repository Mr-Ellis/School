Topic 4 · Output Devices
Year 9 · Hardware · How computers output information

Learning Objectives

  • Understand what is meant by an output device and why it is required
  • Understand how speakers, actuators, LCD screens, LED screens and projectors work
  • Understand the differences between DLP and LCD projectors
💡 Key Fact: Output devices transfer data produced by a computer and turn it into a human-readable form.
🎯 Starter Activity

Think about the computers and devices you use. What output devices do they use to interact with you?

Examples include: Displays and projectors, Printers, Speakers, Actuators.

🔊 Speakers and Headphones

Sound from a computer is produced using a combination of components:

  1. DAC (Digital to Analogue Converter): Converts digital data from the computer into an analogue signal.
  2. Amplifier: Increases the signal strength.
  3. Loudspeaker: Produces the final sound output.
How it works: Digital data → DAC → Amplifier → Speaker → Sound!

⚙️ Actuators

Actuators are output devices used to move mechanisms or systems. They are not the same as motors — a motor spins continuously, while an actuator moves to a specific position.

Think: Give at least three other examples where actuators may be used.

Examples include:

  • Automatic doors (e.g., supermarkets)
  • Central locking on cars
  • Opening and closing valves on pipes
  • Robotic arms (each joint has an actuator)
  • Food and drink manufacturing
  • 3D printer heads

🖥️ LCD Screens

Liquid Crystal Display (LCD) screens are made from millions of pixels (picture elements). Each pixel is made up of three sub-pixels: red, green, and blue.

  • Liquid crystals rotate polarised light to switch pixels on or off.
  • Pixels switch rapidly to create moving colour images.
  • LCD screens need back-lighting because they don't produce their own light.
  • Older LCDs used CCFL (Cold Cathode Fluorescent Lamps).

💡 LED Screens

LED (Light Emitting Diode) screens are very similar to LCD screens. The key difference is the backlighting:

  • LCD screens use a fluorescent backlight (CCFL).
  • LED screens use LEDs to backlight the LCD film.
✅ Advantages of LED backlighting over CCFL:
  • Reach maximum brightness almost instantly.
  • Sharper image with more vivid colours (no yellowish tint).
  • Brighter light → better picture definition.
  • LEDs are very small → screens can be much thinner.
  • Last almost indefinitely → more reliable.
  • Consume very little power → less heat, lower running costs.
Giant LED screens: Use LEDs for each individual sub-pixel. Very bright, suitable for outdoor use (festivals, sports events).

📽️ Projectors

Digital projectors display computer output on larger screens for audiences. There are two common types:

  • DLP (Digital Light Processing)
  • LCD (Liquid Crystal Display)
🔦 DLP Projectors
  • Use millions of micro-mirrors on a chip (one per pixel).
  • Mirrors tilt towards the light source = ON (bright).
  • Mirrors tilt away from the light source = OFF (dark).
  • Mirrors switch thousands of times per second to create shades of grey.
  • A colour wheel (red, green, blue filters) spins in front of the light source.
  • Our brain blends rapid colour changes (e.g., red + blue = purple).
🖼️ LCD Projectors
  • Use a very bright white light source.
  • Light passes through dichroic mirrors to split into red, green, and blue.
  • Each colour passes through a separate LCD screen containing the image.
  • The three colour images are combined using a prism.
  • The full colour image is projected through a lens onto the screen.

🔁 Plenary – Which Device?

Discuss the answers to the following questions with a partner:

  1. Explain the differences between an LED screen and an LCD screen.
  2. Explain the differences between a DLP projector and an LCD projector.
  3. Give four ways in which actuators are used in your life.
Suggested answers:
  • LED vs LCD: Both use LCD film, but LED screens use LEDs for backlighting (vs fluorescent for LCD). Giant LED screens use LEDs for each pixel.
  • DLP vs LCD projectors: DLP uses micro-mirrors and a colour wheel; LCD uses three LCD screens and a prism to combine colours.
  • Actuators: Automatic doors, car door locks, valves, rollercoasters, robotic arms, 3D printers, etc.
