🛡️ Cyber Security Solutions — Flip the Cards

Click each card to flip it and reveal what it does.

Anti-malware

Click to flip →
What it does

Detects and removes malware (viruses, worms, Trojans, spyware). Compares files against virus definitions and quarantines suspicious files.

Encryption

Click to flip →
What it does

Scrambles data so it can't be read without the encryption key. Used for stored passwords, hard drive storage, and website communications.

SSL / HTTPS

Click to flip →
What it does

SSL ensures cryptography is used when transmitting web pages. It makes HTTPS secure — shown by the padlock icon in your browser.

Firewalls

Click to flip →
What it does

Separates a trusted network from an untrusted network (like the Internet). Checks packets and drops those that don't match filtering rules — this is packet filtering.

Access Levels

Click to flip →
What it does

Different users get different permissions. Files may be set as read (open), write (edit/delete) or execute (run). Limits who can do what.

Proxy Servers

Click to flip →
What it does

Makes requests on your behalf so your IP address is hidden. VPNs use proxy servers. Useful on public Wi-Fi to avoid man-in-the-middle attacks.

Privacy Settings

Click to flip →
What it does

Turn off features to reduce risk: microphone, webcam, location data, website tracking and cookies.

Strong Passwords

Click to flip →
What it does

Minimum length, mix of uppercase/lowercase/symbols/numbers, changed regularly. Not based on names, dictionary words or holidays.

Biometrics

Click to flip →
What it does

Measures physical characteristics to verify identity: fingerprint, facial recognition, retinal scan, palm vein, ear, voice.

Auto Updates

Click to flip →
What it does

Automatically patches security holes as soon as they're found. Keeps anti-virus and operating systems up to date.
