The "One Rule" Hackathon

Junior Challenge: Choose a technology, find the mechanism, and build a working browser version.

The One Rule

"A first-time user, with no help from your team, has to understand your idea within two minutes of opening your project."

20 Micro:bit Ideas (Or Pitch Your Own!)

These ideas are categorized by complexity. Remember, you are not limited to this list—if you have a brilliant idea that fits the rule, go for it!

Easy

1. The "Ghost" Touchscreen

Explain how phones detect touch. User touches a foil wire connected to the Micro:bit, and the browser shows a glowing ripple on a virtual phone screen.

Tech: Capacitance | Time: 2-3 hrs
Easy

2. The Automatic Door

Explain supermarket door sensors. Move the Micro:bit closer to the receiver, and the browser's sliding glass doors open with a "Welcome" sound.

Tech: Proximity/Radio | Time: 2-3 hrs
Easy

3. The "Magic" Light Switch

Explain automatic streetlights. Shine a flashlight on the Micro:bit LED grid, and the browser simulates a dark street lighting up.

Tech: Photoresistor | Time: 2 hrs
Easy

4. The Seesaw Balance

Explain torque and center of mass. Tilt the Micro:bit left or right, and a seesaw on the browser tilts, sliding a character off.

Tech: Accelerometer | Time: 2 hrs
Easy

5. The "Invisible" Barcode

Explain light reflection. Wave the Micro:bit over a printed barcode. The browser logs "1"s and "0"s and reveals a grocery price tag.

Tech: Light Sensor | Time: 3 hrs
Easy

6. The "Hot" Mug

Explain thermistors. Pinch the Micro:bit temperature sensor. The browser shows a mug steaming and a digital temperature gauge rising.

Tech: Temp Sensor | Time: 1-2 hrs
Easy

7. The "Wobble" Alarm

Explain car anti-theft systems. Shake the Micro:bit, and the browser shows a car alarm flashing with a "TILT DETECTED" warning.

Tech: Accelerometer | Time: 2 hrs
Easy

8. The "Secret" Knocking Code

Explain smart door locks. Tap the Micro:bit touch pins in a specific pattern. The browser lock only opens if the sequence matches.

Tech: Touch Pins | Time: 3 hrs
Medium

9. The "Muscle" Servo Walker

Explain how human muscles move. A browser slider controls a Micro:bit servo motor attached to a cardboard leg, while the screen shows muscle fibers stretching.

Tech: Servo Motor | Time: 4-5 hrs
Medium

10. Anti-Lock Brakes (ABS)

Explain kinetic friction. Press a browser button. The Micro:bit pulses a wheel, and the browser shows a car stopping smoothly vs. skidding.

Tech: Servo + Accelerometer | Time: 4-5 hrs
Medium

11. The "Heartbeat" Pulse

Explain smartwatch health sensors. Cover the Micro:bit LED with your finger. The browser shows a beating heart animation synced to the sensor.

Tech: Light + LED | Time: 4 hrs
Medium

12. The "Wind" Turbine

Explain kinetic energy. Blow on a pinwheel attached to a sensor. The browser shows a turbine spinning and a battery charging bar.

Tech: Accelerometer + Servo | Time: 5 hrs
Medium

13. The "Earthquake" Detector

Explain seismographs. Shake the Micro:bit at different intensities. The browser shows a Richter scale and a simulated building shaking.

Tech: Accelerometer | Time: 4 hrs
Medium

14. Smart Plant Watering

Explain soil conductivity. Dip Micro:bit pins in wet vs. dry soil. The browser shows a plant wilting or perking up with water droplets.

Tech: Moisture Sensor | Time: 4-5 hrs
Medium

15. Motion Security Light

Explain PIR sensors. Wave your hand in front of the sensor. The browser shows a dark room lighting up and a "MOTION DETECTED" alert.

Tech: PIR Sensor | Time: 4 hrs
Medium

16. The "Speed" Radar Gun

Explain the Doppler effect. Move an object toward an ultrasonic sensor. The browser calculates speed and gives a speeding car a ticket.

Tech: Ultrasonic Sensor | Time: 5 hrs
Hard

17. "Gesture" TV Remote

Explain pattern recognition. Teach the Micro:bit three gestures (tilt left, right, shake) to control a smart TV menu on the browser.

Tech: Accelerometer + ML | Time: 8+ hrs
Hard

18. The "Lie Detector"

Explain polygraphs. Hold the Micro:bit while answering questions. A browser needle jumps based on combined touch, temperature, and motion data.

Tech: Multi-Sensor | Time: 8+ hrs
Hard

19. Self-Driving Car Sim

Explain autonomous vehicles. Use two Micro:bits (car + remote). The browser shows a top-down road where the car avoids obstacles.

Tech: Ultrasonic + Radio | Time: 10+ hrs
Hard

20. Smart Home Hub

Explain sensor fusion. A browser dashboard shows a house where light controls lights, temp controls AC, and motion controls security.

Tech: Multi-Sensor + Web | Time: 12+ hrs
