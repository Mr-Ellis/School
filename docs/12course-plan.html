Term 1 — A1 Computer Fundamentals

A1 Computer Fundamentals Core + HL

Students build a clear understanding of computer architecture, data representation, logic, and operating systems. Covers CPU, GPU, memory, compression, and system software.

Note: A1.2 Data Representation & Logic was covered in Year 11 (2025–26). It will be re-visited in Year 12 starting 2027–28. For the 2026–27 cohort, this topic is omitted from the Year 12 course plan.
HL Note: HL students attend all SL sessions plus 2 additional HL sessions each week. SL students do not attend HL sessions.
Session structure: Each week runs 2 SL sessions (whole class) + 2 HL sessions (HL only).
