Year 12 Course Plan
Full Scope & Sequence · ISR 2026–27 · HL/SL
Term 1 — A1 Computer Fundamentals

A1 Computer Fundamentals Core + HL

Students build a clear understanding of computer architecture, data representation, logic, and operating systems. Covers CPU, GPU, memory, compression, and system software.

Week Dates Topic / Lesson Content 1 Aug 26–30 CPU Architecture · GPU CPU components, registers; GPU role & architecture; AI Strand 2 – Data, Bias & Model Evaluation 2 Sep 2–6 Secondary Memory · Pipelining (HL) SSD/HDD/eMMC, optical, flash; multi‑core, pipelining 3 Sep 9–13 Primary Memory · Fetch-Decode-Execute Memory/register interaction; FDE cycle 4 Sep 16–20 Data Representation 1 & 2 Binary, hex, two's complement 5 Sep 23–27 Data Compression · Cloud Computing RLE, lossless compression; cloud models 6 Sep 30 – Oct 4 Logic Gates · Truth Tables AND/OR/NOT/NAND/NOR/XOR; truth tables 7 Oct 7–11 Logic Diagrams Logic diagram interpretation 8 Oct 14–18 Operating & Control Systems · Translation OS, control systems; compilers/interpreters 🍂 Autumn Break: Oct 19–23
Term 1 — A2 Networks

A2 Networks Core + HL

Students gain understanding of network types, devices, topologies, protocols, addressing, and security. Emphasis on layered communication, packet flow, and architecture comparison.

Week Dates Topic / Lesson Content 9 Oct 26–30 Network Types · Digital Infrastructures LAN/WAN/PAN/VPN; cloud, distributed systems 10 Nov 3–7 Devices & Media · Topologies Routers, switches, fibre, wireless; star, mesh, hybrid 11 Nov 10–14 TCP/IP · Protocols (HL) · Network Models TCP/IP, HTTP, DNS, DHCP; OSI model; network programming 12 Nov 17–21 Network Segmentation · Servers (HL) Segmentation; DNS/DHCP/file/mail/web servers 13 Nov 24–27 IP Addressing · Packet Switching Addressing, packet switching 14 Dec 1–5 Static/Dynamic Routing · Firewalls Routing, firewall design; AI Strand 6 – Ethics & Responsible AI 15 Dec 8–12 Network Vulnerabilities · Countermeasures · Encryption Vulnerabilities, countermeasures, encryption, certificates ❄️ Winter Break: Dec 21 – Jan 1
Term 2 — A4 Machine Learning & Case Study

A4 Machine Learning HL

Students develop practical and conceptual understanding of ML: data prep, regression, classification, clustering, neural networks, and ethical implications.

Week Dates Topic / Lesson Content 16 Dec 15–19 Types of ML · ML Hardware AI Strand 3 – ML models & algorithms; hardware acceleration 17 Jan 4–9 Data Cleaning · Feature Selection (HL) Cleaning, missing data, normalization; filter/wrapper/embedded 18 Jan 11–17 Dimensionality Reduction (HL) · Linear Regression (HL) PCA, curse of dimensionality; predictor/response, slope, r² 19 Jan 18–24 Classification (HL) · Model Metrics (HL) KNN, decision trees; accuracy, precision, recall, F1 20 Jan 25–31 Clustering (HL) · Reinforcement & Genetic Alg. K‑means, segmentation; RL, genetic algorithms 21 Feb 1–7 ANNs · CNNs AI Strand 4 – Neural Networks, Generative AI & LLMs; CNN image interpretation 22 Feb 15–21 Model Selection Model selection, cross‑validation 23 Feb 22–28 Ethics of Emerging Tech Ethical implications of ML; daily life impact
Paper 3 Case Study

Case Study HL

In‑depth analysis of the IB Paper 3 case study: architecture, data flow, stakeholders, constraints, and scenario‑based reasoning.

Week Dates Topic / Lesson Content 24 Mar 1–7 System Overview Architecture, actors, data flow; AI Strand 1 – Understanding AI & Human‑AI Interaction 25 Mar 8–12 Scenario Analysis Inputs, processes, outputs 26 Mar 16–21 Paper 3 Practice Structured responses, reasoning
