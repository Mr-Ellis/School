Profile

Before that, I worked at the esteemed International School of Brussels, and my first step into international teaching was at The Edron Academy in Mexico City, where I worked for three years. I enjoy getting to know my students, understanding how they learn, and helping them grow not only academically but personally. I am passionate about Computer Science and committed to continually improving my practice. I enjoy working with colleagues, sharing ideas, and contributing to a positive, collaborative school culture. I take pride in being someone who can be relied upon—supportive, approachable, and genuinely invested in the success of the whole community.

mr-ellis.github.io/School/unit8-2/lesson3.html

A lesson from Unit 8.2 (Pseudocode & Algorithms) in which students use micro:bits to explore radio communication, encode and decode messages using alphabet positions, and apply conditional logic, test plans and prediction of algorithm outcomes. The lesson also makes explicit links to real-world encryption and the rule-based pattern matching that underpins machine learning.

Employment History

ISR International School on the Rhine

Computer Science Teacher, Grades 7–12  |  08/2020 – Current

I teach Computer Science across Grades 7–12 at ISR International School on the Rhine, delivering a rigorous and forward-thinking curriculum that prepares students for Cambridge IGCSE and the IB Diploma Programme. My work focuses on academic excellence, curriculum alignment, and fostering strong computational thinking and problem-solving skills.

In Grades 7 and 8, students follow the Cambridge Lower Secondary Computing syllabus, which provides a solid foundation in programming, data representation, networks, and digital systems. This programme aligns closely with the Cambridge IGCSE Computer Science (0478) course, ensuring a smooth transition into Upper School and supporting consistent progression in core computing concepts.

My teaching practice is grounded in differentiation and inclusive pedagogy. I use tiered tasks, paired programming, adaptive learning platforms, and targeted feedback to ensure that students of all abilities make meaningful progress. This approach has contributed to strong academic outcomes, with students in both IB and Cambridge Computer Science achieving results that meet or exceed global averages.

Beyond classroom teaching, I lead a range of enrichment opportunities, including robotics workshops, AI clubs, and hackathon preparation. I also organise industry-linked learning experiences, such as visits to Infosys’ Strategic Innovation Center in Düsseldorf, where students explore cutting-edge applications of AI, autonomous systems, and immersive technologies.

I am committed to continuous professional development and actively participate in conferences, workshops, and online computing education communities. My work contributes to ISR’s reputation for academic rigour, innovation, and alignment with internationally recognised qualifications.
