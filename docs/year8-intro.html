Course Overview

This course builds on the foundational principles of computing, exploring topics such as network structure and security, pseudocode and algorithms, computer systems and data representation, game development, data collection and validation, and software design and development. Throughout the year, students will investigate core computing areas including algorithm design, flowcharting, Python programming, database management, and the ethical use of technology.

Emphasis is placed on developing problem-solving strategies, logical reasoning, and digital fluency. Students engage in hands-on activities, including programming in Python, designing smart solutions, and modelling real-world scenarios using data tools. They will also explore how AI and automation are integrated into modern computing systems.

By the end of the course students will have strengthened analytical thinking, engaged in practical computing tasks, and deepened their understanding of algorithmic logic, data representation, digital communication, and the role of computing in society.

Course Topics & Units

Unit 8.1 · Building networks: Network structure and security
  • Identify types of network: PAN, LAN, WAN (8DC.01)
  • Advantages and disadvantages of wired and wireless networks (8DC.03)
  • Echo checks for error detection in transmission (8DC.04)
  • Purpose of operating systems (8CS.01)
  • Role and importance of firewalls in networks (8DC.05)
  • Use of antivirus and anti-spyware to keep data secure (8DC.06)
  • AI Strand 6: AI‑powered security tools detect unusual network behaviour
Unit 8.2 · It's all in the planning: Pseudocode and algorithms
  • Follow and understand algorithms presented as pseudocode (8CT.01)
  • Follow flowcharts and pseudocode with conditional statements (8CT.02)
  • Identify characteristics of pseudocode: short, clear, precise (8CT.03)
  • Describe and use linear searches (8CT.05)
  • Predict outcomes of algorithms and test they meet outcomes (8CT.07)
  • Decompose problems into sub-problems (8CT.08)
  • Identify and describe data types: Integer, Real, Boolean (8P.02)
  • Develop text-based programs with conditional (selection) statements (8P.04)
  • AI Strand 3: Boolean logic linked to ML decision boundaries
Unit 8.3 · Let's talk technology: What's going on inside
  • Purpose of operating systems and utility programs (8CS.01, 8CS.02)
  • ASCII representation of characters (8CS.03)
  • Convert binary to denary and denary to binary (8CS.05)
  • Complete truth tables for AND, OR, NOT gates (8CS.06)
  • Roles of primary memory, RAM and ROM (8CS.07)
  • Compression and why it is required (8CS.04)
  • Machine learning, augmented reality, autonomous programming and AI in robotics (8CS.08, 8CS.09, 8CS.10)
  • AI Strand 4: ML, AR, and autonomous robotics decision-making
Unit 8.4 · Testing conditions: Developing games
  • Decompose problems into sub-problems (8CT.08)
  • Follow and understand algorithms presented as pseudocode (8CT.01)
  • Predict outcomes of algorithms and test they meet outcomes (8CT.07)
  • Develop text-based programs with conditional statements (8P.04)
  • Use an iterative process to develop programs (8P.07)
  • Identify and describe data types: Integer, Real, Boolean (8P.02)
  • Develop algorithms using at least one constant (8CT.09)
  • AI Strand 5: Students examine how simple game AIs use conditional logic
Unit 8.5 · Data input: Data collection and validation
  • Identify key features of models such as simulators, including data requirements (8MD.01)
  • Design appropriate forms, including data validation, to collect data (8MD.02)
  • Use spreadsheets as models of real-life systems using what-if analysis (8MD.03)
  • Evaluate suitability of data collected for particular purposes (8MD.04)
  • Add validation rules to a database structure (8MD.05)
  • Evaluate suitability of pre-existing databases for given purposes (8MD.06)
  • Identify data required for specific applications (8MD.07)
  • AI Strand 1 & 2: Understanding AI & Human-AI Interaction; Data, Bias & Model Evaluation
Unit 8.6 · Iterating through a solution: Software design and development
  • Decompose problems into sub-problems (8CT.08)
  • Follow and understand algorithms presented as pseudocode (8CT.01)
  • Understand and use rules using AND, OR and NOT (8CT.06)
  • Identify and describe data types: Integer, Real, Boolean (8P.02)
  • Develop text-based programs using data types (8P.05)
  • Use an iterative process to develop programs (8P.07)
  • Outline purpose of program libraries (8P.01)
  • Develop and apply test plans (8P.08)
  • AI Highlight: Pseudocode used to describe AI decision‑making (rule‑based agents)
