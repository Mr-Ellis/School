📌 Learning Objective

🔑 Key Terms

TermDefinition
PAN (Personal Area Network)A network of devices connected within the immediate vicinity of a person (e.g., Bluetooth earphones to a phone).
LAN (Local Area Network)A network covering a small geographic area, such as a home, office, or school. High speed, owned by a single entity.
WAN (Wide Area Network)A network covering a large geographic area, connecting multiple LANs (e.g., the Internet). Uses external infrastructure (telecom lines).
VPN (Virtual Private Network)Creates a secure, encrypted connection over an untrusted network (like the internet), providing privacy and anonymity.
BandwidthThe maximum rate of data transfer across a given path, usually measured in Mbps (megabits per second).
LatencyThe time delay taken for a data packet to travel from source to destination.
TopologyThe physical or logical arrangement of devices in a network (e.g., Star, Bus, Mesh).
Client/Server ModelA network where one central computer (server) provides resources and services to other computers (clients).
Peer-to-Peer (P2P)A network where all computers have equal status and can act as both client and server, sharing resources directly.
📌 EXAM TIP: When asked to compare LAN and WAN, focus on geographic scale, ownership (private vs. third-party), and transmission media (owned cables vs. leased telecom lines).
