Course Aims

Building on the foundations established in Year 9, this course continues the IGCSE Computer Science syllabus (Topics 1–10) across the year, with dedicated time for revision and external exams.

  • Topics 1–6: Computer Systems (Paper 1)
  • Topics 7–10: Algorithms, Programming and Logic (Paper 2)
  • AI Integration: 6 AI strands embedded throughout the course, exploring human-AI interaction, machine learning, ethics, and more.

Duration: 29 Weeks + Revision

AI Integration Across the Course

Six AI strands are embedded throughout the Year 10 course to prepare students for the emerging technologies section of the syllabus:

  • AI Strand 1 (Week 1): Understanding AI & Human-AI Interaction – Scanners, cameras, and OCR as human-AI input systems.
  • AI Strand 2 (Week 19): Data, Bias & Model Evaluation – Analysing CSV data and understanding how data quality affects outcomes.
  • AI Strand 3 (Week 12): Machine Learning Models & Algorithms – Evaluating algorithm behaviour using test data and trace logic.
  • AI Strand 4 (Week 26): Neural Networks, Generative AI & LLMs – Exploring how hardware supports multi-step decision systems.
  • AI Strand 5 (Week 30+): Creative & Applied AI – Designing interactive game systems using variables, conditions, and physics.
  • AI Strand 6 (Week 6): Ethics, Society & Responsible AI – Analysing safe browsing, secure protocols, and responsible digital use.

Exam Information (0478)

2026-2028 Assessment Overview

  • Paper 1 – Computer Systems 1 hour 45 mins | 75 marks (50%)
    Covers Topics 1–6 (Short-answer and structured questions).
  • Paper 2 – Algorithms, Programming & Logic 1 hour 45 mins | 75 marks (50%)
    Covers Topics 7–10 (Includes a 15-mark scenario question).
  • NO Calculators permitted Candidates are expected to be able to perform binary and hex conversions manually.
  • Programming Language For the scenario question, candidates may use pseudocode.
