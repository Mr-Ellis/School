Digital Learning Platform & Resource Library

I do not just teach the curriculum; I build the resources to deliver it. I created and maintain mr-ellis.github.io/School, a comprehensive website that houses my entire curriculum library. It serves as the primary resource hub for all my lessons and is regularly shared with colleagues. Below is a selection of materials illustrating my pedagogical approach:

IB Computer Science — A1.3.3 Scheduling

mr-ellis.github.io/School/A1/A1.3.3-summary.html

Pedagogy: Teaching exam technique through gamification. Students take on the role of competing tech teams pitching CPU scheduling algorithms to Microsoft. The lesson breaks down the "Compare" command term using structured sentence frames, ensuring students move from simple description to Level 4 comparative analysis.

IB Computer Science — B2.3.4 Functions (Lab)

mr-ellis.github.io/School/B2/G11/b2-3-4-lab.html

Pedagogy: Interactive, scaffolded practical learning. Features an embedded Python IDE for immediate testing, a Parsons game to reinforce logic sequencing, and an AI-powered Feynman technique extension to test conceptual understanding.

Key Stage 3 Computing — Radio Communication & Encoding

mr-ellis.github.io/School/unit8-2/lesson3.html

Pedagogy: Physical computing and cross-curricular links. Students use micro:bits to explore radio communication, encode/decode messages, and apply conditional logic. The lesson makes explicit links to real-world encryption and machine learning.
